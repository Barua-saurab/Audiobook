The Art of Building Simple Things

When we first start building things—whether it is writing an essay, writing code, or taking a photo—we tend to focus on the essentials. But over time, complexity creeps in. We add more tools, more frameworks, and more rules.

Real elegance is not about adding more; it is about removing everything that distracts from the core message. When you write on a blank page without digital clutter, your thoughts flow more naturally.

By keeping our tools light and simple, we spend less time managing the system and more time doing the actual work that matters.
